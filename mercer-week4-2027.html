DANIEL MERCER · WEEK 4

Four Wins. One Devastating Loss.

Price powers Oklahoma City past Tampa Bay; Overshown’s injury changes the defensive outlook.

OKLAHOMA CITY — For three weeks, the Oklahoma City Bison had been finding ways to win. Against Tampa Bay, they looked like a team that knew exactly how it wanted to win. Then came the injury.

The Bison defeated the Buccaneers 27–17 behind 250 rushing yards, efficient quarterback play, and a defense that intercepted Baker Mayfield. But DeMarvion Overshown suffered a torn rotator cuff that will sideline him for 18 games.

Price is becoming the identity

Jadarian Price carried 23 times for 193 yards and a touchdown, averaging 8.4 yards per attempt, with a long run of 41. Trey Benson added 46 yards and a touchdown on seven carries. Price finished without a fumble, keeping Marshawn Lynch's Lamborghini wager alive.

The Bison are no longer simply running to set up the pass. They are asking defenses to prove they can stop the ground game.

Howard finally got some protection

After seven sacks against Houston, Oklahoma City allowed just one against Tampa Bay. Howard completed 15 of 18 passes for 143 yards, a touchdown and no interceptions. Tyler Warren caught three passes for 53 yards and a touchdown.

Overshown's lasting impact

Before leaving with the injury, Overshown made nine tackles and intercepted Mayfield. Leonard Williams registered 1.5 sacks and Yaya Diaby added half a sack. The Bison allowed only 17 points and forced the game's only interception.

With CJ Allen still on injured reserve, Overshown's loss makes linebacker depth an immediate personnel problem, not a theoretical one.

Winning is getting harder

Oklahoma City is 4–0, with a run game opponents have struggled to contain. Yet staying healthy enough to preserve this start may be the greater challenge.

Mercer's Verdict: Four wins. One devastating loss.
