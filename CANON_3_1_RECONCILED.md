### BMW retrieval arc

Bryan originally owned and extensively modified his 2015 M235i. It accompanied him, Q, and Kona during their earlier life, including the Seattle-to-Oklahoma move.

He sold the car to help finance his ICT firm, not to fund the Bison. This distinction is important: Bryan was already extremely wealthy when he acquired the NFL franchise.

Later, Bryan tracked down his exact BMW by VIN. It was owned by Marcus Bennett in Fort Worth, with approximately 121,000 miles. Bryan negotiated a purchase around $24,800, including the loan payoff, and recovered the original car with much of its modified setup intact.

### FK8 retrieval arc

The 2019 Civic Type R accompanied Bryan on the Seattle-to-Oklahoma move with Jonathan, multiple pets, luggage, and computer equipment.

After the move, long commutes and demanding work schedules made the Civic less practical. Bryan traded it toward Q's 2024 Mach-E.

Years later, Andy found the original Type R in Edmond, owned by Ethan Walker. It had approximately 68,400 miles, its Eventuri intake, and evidence of earlier tuning. After inspection, Bryan bought it for $35,000. Ethan also received Bison merchandise and game tickets.

### Documentary significance

Bryan told Mina and Alex both retrieval stories and revealed that the Mach-E will eventually belong to Braylee.

Mina recognized that the BMW, FK8, and Mach-E tell a more meaningful story than the GT3 RS and Rolls-Royces: they represent entrepreneurship, sacrifices, family decisions, and continuity between Bryan's past and present.

Alex plans to structure the garage footage around that contrast.

The crew also discovered the framed photograph of the Urus, prompting jokes about Marshawn potentially winning both the Lamborghini and its picture.
