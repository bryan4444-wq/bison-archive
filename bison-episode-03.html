The Weight of Winning

Mina Kimes's third Bison documentary follows the Texans and Buccaneers games, the physical price of an undefeated start, and the lives surrounding the franchise. Alex produces; Marcus films.

Family, memory, and the garage

November 1 footage includes Q insisting the crew eat breakfast, Vietnamese family cooking stories, Braylee, and a garage tour centered on the original green M235i, white FK8 Type R and family Mach-E. The BMW was sold for ICT payroll; the FK8 was traded toward the Mach-E. Both original cars were later recovered.

Braylee's Gift

Bryan announces a proposed five-student annual Oklahoma scholarship program covering full tuition and mandatory fees, with academic merit and financial need. Applications are expected to open December 2027; awards have not yet been made.

Four and zero

The episode covers Houston's seven sacks of Howard, Price's 193-yard Tampa Bay game and Overshown's rotator-cuff injury. Bryan requested a hard cut to black after the Week 4 postgame statement and the 4–0 graphic.

The private screening is scheduled for Wednesday, November 4, time TBD. This page is a verified episode guide, not an invented transcript or a claim that the screening already occurred. Derek/Bryan postgame material is held for Episode 4.
