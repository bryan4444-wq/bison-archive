DANIEL MERCER · WEEK 3

The Bison Are 3–0. Their Offensive Line Is Giving Them Every Reason to Worry.

Houston outgained Oklahoma City, but two Stroud interceptions and an efficient Howard kept the Bison undefeated.

OKLAHOMA CITY — The Oklahoma City Bison are undefeated. Their quarterback is efficient. Their running backs are finding the end zone. Their defense is generating pressure.

And somehow, after a 24–19 victory over Houston, there are more questions about this team's offense than there were before kickoff.

Will Howard completed 16 of 19 passes for 160 yards, one touchdown and no interceptions. He was also sacked seven times. Seven.

Houston controlled the statistical battle: 468 total yards to Oklahoma City's 330, 17 first downs to 12, and more than five additional minutes of possession. But C.J. Stroud threw two interceptions, and Oklahoma City didn't throw any.

The quarterback is holding up his end

Howard completed more than 84 percent of his passes while absorbing constant pressure. Tetairoa McMillan caught eight passes for 112 yards, giving his quarterback a reliable target even when protection failed. There is a difference between demonstrating toughness and being forced to demonstrate it every Sunday.

A running game that still matters

Jadarian Price had 76 yards and a touchdown on 16 carries; Trey Benson added 38 yards and a score on five carries. Price did not fumble. The two backs combined for 114 rushing yards and two touchdowns.

Defense answers

Camryn Bynum led the team with 11 tackles. Ashton Hampton was credited with an interception, and Adetomiwa Adebawore recorded two sacks. Houston still moved the football, but Oklahoma City generated enough disruptive plays to survive.

Winning isn't the same as being finished

Shane Steichen's team is 3–0, and the record is deserved. But seven sacks cannot become normal. This is the kind of warning a team should heed while it is still winning.

Mercer's Verdict: Three wins, zero losses, and one very serious protection problem.
