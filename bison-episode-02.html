MINA KIMES DOCUMENTARY · EPISODE TWO · PRIVATE SCREENING OCTOBER 24, 2027

BISON — Episode Two

The revised definitive screening cut · Indianapolis to Oklahoma City · The price of belief

The quarterback who arrived as QB1

Archival Indianapolis footage traces Will Levis's 0–7 start in 2026 and Will Howard taking over the Colts' starting job. Howard arrived in Oklahoma City already established as QB1. The documentary revisits the Bison's Week 1 victory and their 2–0 start.

Marshawn and the Lamborghini

After Jadarian Price fumbled twice in Week 1, Marshawn Lynch arrived in Week 2 to coach ball security. Bryan's Urus wager begins in Week 2: no fumbles by Price for the remainder of the regular season, with playoffs excluded. The 191-yard, two-touchdown Jacksonville performance kept the wager alive.

63–10: The Madden massacre

Trey Benson's 63–10 Madden victory over Price becomes the documentary's comic centerpiece. The team teases JP despite his on-field resurgence.

What it cost

The episode shows Bryan's recovered green 2015 BMW M235i and white 2019 Civic Type R FK8. The BMW was sold to support payroll at Bryan's ICT company; the FK8 was traded toward the family Mach-E. The two original cars were later located and bought back. They represent different sacrifices, not two cars sold for the same reason.

The people who built it

The documentary features Bobby Wagner, Shane Steichen, Sarah, Pete Carroll, and Patricia's Seat, honoring Bryan's late mother. The private screening aboard the Bison jet concludes with Mina seeking Bryan's reaction.

This is a continuity-accurate episode guide, not a verbatim reproduction of the full owner-supplied screenplay. Episode Three will cover Weeks 3–4 and the later November 1 family/garage footage.
