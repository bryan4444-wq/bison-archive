Free agency

Four headline additions

Yaya Diaby, edge rusher and 2026 15.5-sack producer; Parker Washington, a dynamic wideout; Leonard “Big Cat” Williams, veteran defensive tackle and Bobby Wagner’s former teammate; and Ikem Ekwonu, offensive tackle. The moves aimed to accelerate the window rather than wait for a multiyear rebuild.

Original agreements recorded: Diaby four years/$70M; Washington four years/$68M; Williams two years/$22M; Ekwonu four years/$47M. Later Madden contract screens can differ and are preserved separately under Team Ops.

2027 draft

Three first-round swings

No. 6 Jeremiah Smith (WR), No. 14 Trevor Goosby (OT), and No. 19 Ashton Hampton (CB). Later selections: Damon Wilson II, Nyck Harbor, Rasheem Biles, Isaiah Sategna, and John Mateer.

Arizona deal: pick 6 and Zaven Collins for Laiatu Latu plus picks 27 and 110. Detroit deal: pick 19 for picks 32, 52, and 142. The Smith selection was real canon despite a Madden draft-recap display glitch.

Offensive identity

Everything around Will Howard

Howard took over an 0–7 team and finished 8–2 as a starter, throwing for 2,403 yards, 14 touchdowns and five interceptions. He enters 2027 as QB1 with Tetairoa McMillan, Parker Washington, Jeremiah Smith, Nyck Harbor and Tyler Warren.

The departures of Tory Horton and Andrei Iosivas mattered to Howard personally. His players-only throwing session was approved but had not yet occurred at this archive snapshot.

Defense

Talent, versatility and unanswered questions

Diaby joins an emerging defensive group with Sauce Gardner, Cam Taylor-Britt, Ashton Hampton, Leonard Williams, Gervon Dexter Sr., Zaven Collins, Danny Stutsman and CJ Allen.

The defense looks stronger on paper, but interior depth, linebacker balance and rookie roles remain worth watching.

Front office

Bold decisions carry costs

Owner Bryan Luong, Bobby Wagner, Scot McCloughan, Pete Carroll and Shane Steichen must turn aggressive trades and signings into a coherent team. Questions remain around draft-capital expenditure, offensive-line chemistry and roster depth.
