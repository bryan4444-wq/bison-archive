Independent Bison Beat

From relocation experiment to contender

Daniel Mercer • April 2027

There was a time, not particularly long ago, when the biggest question surrounding this franchise was whether Oklahoma City could support an NFL team. Now the question is whether Oklahoma City can win a Super Bowl.

Four major free-agent acquisitions, three first-round draft picks, and a young quarterback who went 8–2 as a starter have transformed the outlook. Yet ambition is not a substitute for results.

The Will Howard question

Howard's 2026 turnaround was extraordinary. With Tetairoa McMillan, Parker Washington, Jeremiah Smith, Nyck Harbor, and Tyler Warren available, he now has the resources to prove he can lead a contender across a full season.

The ownership gamble

Bryan Luong has shown that he is willing to spend, trade, and take risks. The best organizations also understand restraint. Whether his aggressive decisions add up to a cohesive football team is the defining question of the season.

He isn't waiting for the organization to tell him it's ready. He's trying to make it ready.

Mercer's grade: A−. Projected wins: 10–11. This page is an archival excerpt of the fictional in-universe article, not an actual published report.
